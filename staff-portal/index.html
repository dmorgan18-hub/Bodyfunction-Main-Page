📞 £49 Introductory Offer — Outbound Call Script

Use this script when calling a lead who has just submitted their details for the £49 Introductory Offer (usually £80, Osteopathy only, Angel Islington only). Speed matters — call as soon as the lead lands in Go High Level.

Golden rule: No payment = no booking. The full £49 must be secured as a deposit — either taken over the phone via Stripe, or paid immediately by the patient via a Cliniko SMS payment link — before the appointment is confirmed.
Step 1 — Before you dial
Check Go High Level: confirm the lead is tagged "Introductory Offer" (not the free 15‑minute consult) and note their name, number and what they've told us about their pain/symptoms.
Step 2 — Opening
Hi, is this [Name]? It's [Your Name] calling from Bodyfunction Clinic in Angel — you recently enquired about our £49 introductory osteopathy appointment. Is now a good time for a couple of minutes?
Step 3 — Discovery
Can I ask what's been going on — where's the pain, and how long have you had it for?
Listen, note it down, and use it to reassure them the right practitioner can help.
Step 4 — Explain the offer
So this appointment is normally £80, but as part of our current offer it's just £49. It includes a full consultation, our osteopath finding the root cause of the problem, and hands‑on treatment in the same session — it's a full appointment, with treatment. It runs for about 45 minutes and is only available here at our Angel Islington clinic.
Step 5 — Book the appointment
I can get you booked in with one of our osteopaths at Angel — would earlier or later in the week work better for you?
Book as "Introductory Offer" appointment type in Cliniko, at Angel Islington, with any available osteopath (not physio). Take name, DOB, email, mobile, address and referral source.
Step 6 — Take payment (this is the critical step)
To secure that slot I just need to take the £49 now — would you like to pay over the phone, or would you prefer I text you a secure payment link instead?

Option A — Card over the phone (Stripe):

Whenever you're ready, could you read me your card number, expiry date and the 3‑digit security code?
Key the payment straight into the Stripe virtual terminal as they read it — never write the full card number down on paper. Then in Cliniko: open the appointment → Add Invoice → link the practitioner and appointment date → select Consultation Deposit as the invoice item → Create Invoice → add the deposit amount against Credit Card → save the payment, and assign the invoice to the correct practitioner.

Option B — Cliniko payment link by SMS (if they'd rather not read their card out):

No problem at all — I'll text you a secure payment link right now. It'll take you about 30 seconds, and you can pay by card, or just tap to pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay if that's quicker and more secure for you. Let me know as soon as it's gone through and I'll lock in your slot.
Send the Cliniko invoice payment link via SMS to the patient's mobile. Apple Pay / Google Pay are supported automatically on the patient's phone for extra security — no card details need to be read aloud. Stay on the line if possible, or agree a callback, to confirm the payment has landed before treating the booking as fully confirmed.
If the patient hesitates on payment entirely, do not confirm the appointment. Politely explain the £49 rate only holds the slot once the deposit is paid, and offer to send the payment link so they can complete it in their own time — but keep the diary slot provisional until it's paid.
Step 7 — Confirm & give directions
Perfect, you're all booked in for [day/time] with [practitioner] at our Angel Islington clinic, 10 Barnsbury Road, N1 0HB — that's right by Angel tube, near Angel Police Station. Just ring the buzzer to the left of the door and come on in; there's pay‑and‑display parking on Dewey Road if you're driving. You'll get a confirmation email with a short new patient form — it only takes a couple of minutes and can be done anytime before your appointment, even five minutes before if needed. Could you also arrive about 10 minutes early?
Step 8 — Close
Great, we look forward to seeing you on [day/time]. If anything comes up and you need to change it, just give us a call — we ask for at least 24 hours' notice where possible. Thanks so much, [Name], have a great day!
After the call:
  • Log the call outcome and payment status in Go High Level.
  • Confirm the Cliniko invoice/deposit is fully recorded and assigned to the correct practitioner.
  • Double‑check the appointment type is set to "Introductory Offer" and the location is Angel Islington.
